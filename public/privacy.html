Privacy Policy for Drawing Streak App

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Drawing Streak App (also referred to as "drawing-streak-app"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our application.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Drawing Streak App is designed to help you maintain a daily drawing habit. To provide this functionality, the app integrates with Google Drive. We only collect and use the minimum data necessary for the app to function.

  • Google Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (such as your name and email) solely to authenticate you.
  • Google Drive Data: The app requests permission to create, access, and manage a specific folder within your Google Drive. This is used exclusively to upload and store your drawings and save your application settings. We do not access, view, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your drawings and app settings are stored directly on your personal Google Drive account. We do not store your drawings, personal data, or authentication tokens on any external servers owned by us. All data transmission between the app and Google Drive happens securely over HTTPS directly from your device.

4. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising services that collect your personal information. Your data remains strictly between your device and Google's servers.

5. Changes to Our Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at your earliest convenience.
